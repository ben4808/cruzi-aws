PASS 1
First, consider if the item belongs to any of these buckets, evaluating from top to bottom. 
If it does, assign that bucket and immediately move on to the next item.

Non-unit: Unity bucket is exactly Non-unit or Nonsense -> automatic Non-unit. No other unity bucket triggers this.
Uncommon Derivation: An inflected or derived form that is rarely used in modern natural speech, 
  even if grammatically valid or present in specialized dictionaries (saner, Nates, SNRs, wellnesses, liaises).
  Transparent re-/de- verb forms are not Uncommon Derivations (retyped, reseed, deseed).
Clunky: A non-dominant formulation.
  Ask: "If a native speaker wanted to express exactly this, would they use this exact string?"
  If a different form is clearly standard (Levi → Levi's, PBJ → PB&J, C-cell → C battery, F-keys → function keys,
  as do I → so do I, Macao → Macau), → Clunky.
  Includes contrived or non-standard shortenings (systs, qual), contrived archaic forms (ashine, adance),
  misspellings and contrived variants (schtik, ID'd), and multi-word fragments that dangle or are padded (as ice, is for, a must, the UK).
  Valid alternate or British spellings (analyse, spelt) are not Clunky.
  Single bound words, contractions, and prefixes/suffixes (utero, he'd, osteo-, tri-) are not Clunky.

PASS 2
Again, choose the first bucket that applies, evaluating from top to bottom.

Idiomatic: Non-literal multi-word phrase with a colloquial, informal, or figurative flavor
  (nip at, life or death, oh it's on, act up, did in, a ton, went to pot).
  Does NOT include neutral-register phrasal verbs or functional phrases (end up, take up, at all, per se, log on).
  Does NOT include single words.
Interesting: The thing itself has a story or cultural phenomenon behind it that a curious person would enjoy learning about.
  Good fodder for a movie, book, or trivia question because of that lore. A colorful etymology alone doesn't count (aloof).
  Everyday brands/products whose main draw is enjoyment belong in Feel-Good (Coke).
  Common Names are not Interesting.
Feel-Good: Triggers an immediate pleasant reaction — the kind of reward people feel before thinking about it.
  Includes indulgent foods and treats (pecan, Coke), delight (cute animals), celebration and honors (up top, atta boy, Best Picture),
  enjoyable activities and outings (go on a date, estate sale, salad bar), beloved pastimes (avid reader), comfort, and nostalgia.
  Also includes drinks (latte, IPA), praise people enjoy hearing (amazing, brave, smart), excitement (rev up, came alive, aim high),
  and coolness or style (retro, edgy, backstage).
  NOT plain staples or ingredients (pita, bao), or abstract nouns and feelings that merely name a positive concept (courage, freedom, glad, win).
  Ask: "Does encountering it produce a pleasant reaction, or does it just describe something good?"
Trendy: Using it signals fluency in spoken, colloquial culture — membership in a social or generational in-group.
  Includes texting slang, generational sayings from any era, lifestyle lingo (cheat meal, vape pen, brews), tech buzzwords,
  and political slang (RINOs, neocon).
  Does NOT include terms that have become the standard name for something (app), regionalisms or loanwords (loo, passé),
  field jargon (O-line), or words that are merely informal (yap).

PASS 3
Determine whether these attributes either apply to the item or they don't.

Vulgar: Vulgar and inappropriate to use in formal settings or around children. Includes bodily-function slang (rip one, peepee).
Sensitive: Touches a topic many people would find uncomfortable in polite or mixed company: sexual or bodily connotations
  (erect, teats, C cups), stigmatized health conditions (STDs, UTI, obese), recreational drugs and DUI (opium, THC, DWI),
  hatred or evil (hate, demon, Satan), gender/sexuality/race identity (he/him, racism), partisan politics (RNC, neocon),
  and predatory or demeaning behavior (leers, sneers, hitman).
  Sad or serious subject matter alone is not Sensitive (mourn, elegy, alone, deaths, arson).

DEFAULT
An item that doesn't fit into any bucket from Pass 1 or Pass 2 should be classified as "Normal".

OUTPUT
Output according to the following format, one item per line.
Do not re-output the unity or familiarity buckets.
Don't include any explanatory or auxiliary text.

<input item> : <bucket name> (Vulgar, Sensitive)

Examples:
crown : Normal
George Foreman : Interesting
funnest : Uncommon Derivation
shit-faced : Idiomatic (Vulgar)
obese : Normal (Sensitive)
pecan : Feel-Good
courage : Normal
see ya : Trendy
