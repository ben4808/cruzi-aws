TASK
First, imagine you are game show contestant and you are tasked with seeing 3-letter combinations and shouting out the first 
interpretation that comes to mind. Whoever can produce the most popular interpretation wins. (Family Feud style)

Second, imagine you are a language usage expert tasked with finding the most canonical interpretation of the combination.

Third, you are an expert in Googling things. Do a Google search for the combination and pick the most common interpretation 
from the results.

Perform these exercises with every 3-letter combination starting with "BY".

Then, for each answer, do a couple extra steps:
1. CLASSIFY
- Which one of these classifications was the combination interpreted as? Word, Phrase, Partial Phrase, Proper Name, Acronym/Abbreviation, Prefix/Suffix.
- Write the combination naturally according to the interpreted classification. Don't capitalize the first letter unless it's natural to do so.

2. FREQUENCY
Choose the first bucket that applies.
  - Fundamental: Universally known and understood. Might appear in a children's book.
  - Solid: Well-established and universal in the language.
  - Niche: Particular to a narrow context. Often a Proper Name that isn't well-established in the language.
  - A Stretch: Required creative interpretation and filling in the blanks. Not canonical.

OUTPUT
- The Short Description is a focused description that allows people to easily distinguish it from other interpetataions. 
  This should be as short as possible.
  
Output the results strictly following the given format. Output a blank line between each combination.
Don't include any extra explanatory or auxiliary text. Don't include any images, only text.

<combination>
<game show natural form> : <game show short description> : <game show classification> : <game show frequency bucket>
<canonical natural form> : <canonical short description> : <canonical classification> : <canonical frequency bucket>
<Google natural form> : <Google short description> : <Google classification> : <Google frequency bucket>
