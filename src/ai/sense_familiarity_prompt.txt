TASK
You are given a list of words and phrases along with the senses that they must be interpreted as.

Each item is given as: <item> (<classification>) (<unity bucket>) (<sense summary>)
The classification will be one of:
  Word, Phrase, Proper Name, Acronym/Abbreviation, Prefix/Suffix

The unity bucket will be one of:
  Concept, Collocation, Formula, Partial, Variant, Formulaic, Non-unit, Nonsense

Your task is to classify each item into buckets according to its familiarity.

GUIDELINES
- Rate the familiarity of the given sense, not of the word. A sense does not inherit its word's familiarity
   (pod as "group of seals" is Niche even though pod is common).
- Ubiquitous applies only to the primary, everyday sense of a core high-frequency word — a ubiquitous sense of a polysemous
   core word, the meaning a child would learn first. Secondary or extended senses of core words (fall as "waterfall",
   pass as "mountain route") are rated on their own familiarity.
- For names, score the specific referent described in the sense summary.
   - If the referent is a brand, institution, or famous person, rate that referent (Levi as "jeans company" → General Knowledge).
   - Common Name only if the name is frequent and the referent isn't independently famous (Ann as "Ann Packer" → Common Name).
   - A "Personal Name" sense is Common Name only when the name is genuinely common, shared by a wide variety of people.
     A "Personal Name" sense of an uncommon name gets a different bucket based on how recognizable the name is.
