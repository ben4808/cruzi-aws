INPUT
A list of words together with the sense in which they should be considered.

TASK
For each word, determine whether it is a mass noun when used in the given sense, that is, a noun for which the plural form is rare or non-existent.
For example, "clothing", "cotton", or "mail".

OUTPUT
Output each word that is a mass noun together with its sense, just like it appeared in the input, one per line.
Don't include any introductory, explanatory, or auxiliary text.
