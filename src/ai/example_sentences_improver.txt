PERSONAS
Alice is a creative writing expert, knowledgeable in colloquial and practical American English.
Bob is a linguistics expert and knows proper grammar and common usage patterns. 
Charlie is an expert in literature, poetry, and music, good at finding good quotes for any situation.

TASK
The three personas will be presented with 20 example sentences. Each one will come together with:
1. The word or phrase that the sentence is meant to highlight.
2. The part of speech in which it must be interpreted.
3. The sense in which it must be interpreted. 

Their task is to determine the worst example sentence of the 20 presented and recommend a stellar replacement, leveraging all their creativity and expert knowledge.

Reasons that an example sentence might be considered bad:
1. Awkward: The formulation of the sentence is unnatural -- it uses words in a way that a native speaker wouldn't use them.
2. Unfaithful: It doesn't highlight the exact given sense and part of speech. It might use a slightly different sense or different part of speech.

Guidelines for a good example sentence:
Relevance: Use the word/phrase naturally and accurately, exemplifying it in the exact given sense. The sentence doesn't work without it.
Everyday Focus: Highlight practical, real-life ways the word/phrase might be used in casual conversations, daily routines, work chats,
  family moments, or simple social interactions. Keep it grounded in ordinary scenarios like home, school, work, hobbies, errands, and family.
Simplicity: Use straightforward, easy-to-read language—no fancy words, complex structures, or rare vocabulary. Aim for clear,
  conversational tone that feels like spoken English.
Interest: Add a touch of warmth, humor, surprise, or relatability to make them engaging without overcomplicating. Vary contexts
  (e.g., morning coffee chats, quick texts, neighborhood talks) and tones (e.g., light-hearted, thoughtful, funny).
Quality: Keep sentences concise (6-20 words), grammatically flawless, and polished for readability.

PROCEDURE
1. Simulate a collaborative discussion between Alice, Bob, and Charlie.
2. Have them debate which sentence is the absolute worst based on the Awkward/Unfaithful criteria until they reach a consensus.
3. Have them pitch and refine replacement sentences to ensure they meet all the creative guidelines. Preserve the inflection of the word in the original sentence where possible.
4. Finally, give a translation of the replacement sentence into natural Spanish.

If Alice and Bob decide that all of the given example sentences are already of high quality, simply output "No improvement suggested" in the output summary.

OUTPUT
At the end of the output, include a summary of their findings in this exact format. Don't include any extra introductory or explanatory text in the summary.
IMPORTANT: As it was in the input, enclose the word/phrase in question in {{double braces}} in the English sentence.

WORST SENTENCE
<word/phrase> (<sense>): <sentence>

RECOMMENDED REPLACEMENT
<sentence in English>
<sentence in Spanish>

SENTENCES TO EVALUATE
