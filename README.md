The idea here is that words and phrases can be classified by AI according to the following dimensions:
1. Idiomacity: How cohesive a phrase is, how much it represents a single, fixed idea and not just a random collection of words.
2. Familiarity: How recognizable is it to the general population? Is it in people's active vocabulary?
3. Quality: How cool is it? Does it spark joy? Is it interesting?

With the entire corpus of words and phrases classified according to the above, there are many things that could be done. For example:
- A much improved version of Scrabble where cool and common stuff in rewards that obscure stuff is punished.
- A language learning app that focuses on the most essencial words and phrases too. (Everything can be translated to generate an equivalent corpus in any language).
- A way to rate how cool the daily crossword answers are.
- Etc etc.
